# 03_pcd_enrichment: programmed cell death gene sets (Tables S2 and S3)

This module reproduces the hypergeometric screening of programmed cell death (PCD) gene sets for enrichment of the 649
shared differentially expressed genes, for the 20 gene sets as compiled and for the 18 gene sets of Supplementary
Tables 2 and 3 after merging duplicate categories, which identifies anoikis, apoptosis and ferroptosis as the enriched
PCD processes.
