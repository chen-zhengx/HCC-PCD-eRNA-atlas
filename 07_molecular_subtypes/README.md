# 07_molecular_subtypes: molecular subtypes and their correspondence with risk groups

This module contains the consensus clustering of the 70 GSE144269 tumors into two molecular subtypes based on
PCD-related eRNA expression, and reproduces the correspondence between the subtypes and the high- and low-risk groups
(Figures 3D, S1D and S2D) and the concordance of risk groups and of subtypes across the three PCD models (Figure 5).
